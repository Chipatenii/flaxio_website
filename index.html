About us

Built to support Zambian
mining and industry

Flaxio Investment Limited is a Zambian-owned company committed to delivering reliable industrial solutions to the mining, manufacturing and commercial sectors. We specialise in the supply of sulphuric acid, mining chemicals, drilling equipment, electrical equipment and hazardous waste management services.

With a strong commitment to safety, quality, professionalism and customer satisfaction, we continue to build strategic partnerships across the mining and industrial sectors by providing efficient and dependable service. Our company is driven by integrity, operational excellence and a vision to contribute meaningfully to Zambia's industrial and economic growth.

Our facilities

Hazardous-waste
dumpsite operations

Flaxio operates a dedicated hazardous-waste dumpsite at Itimpi, Kitwe — a designated facility for the secure, environmentally compliant disposal and processing of hazardous waste. A further site at Lufwanyama is planned to expand our regional disposal capacity.

  • Modern treatment techniques that neutralise hazardous compounds
  • Operations held to strict regulatory standards
  • Continuous monitoring to mitigate environmental risk
